Easy on digestion

Most protein bars upset your stomach for three reasons. Goat Bar has none of them.

  • Sugar alcohols. Maltitol and erythritol don't fully absorb. They sit in your gut and ferment. That's the gas and the bloat.
  • Fiber syrups. Chicory root and soluble corn fiber ferment the same way.
  • Soy and whey. Soy carries sugars that ferment. Whey concentrate carries lactose.

Goat Bar is built on goat casein instead. It forms a softer curd in your stomach than cow's milk protein, and it's naturally A2-type.

2

Energy for longer

Whey hits fast and is mostly gone in 3 to 5 hours. Goat casein releases protein slowly, over about 7 hours.

  • Slow release. Steady fuel from the trailhead to the top, not a spike and a fade.
  • No sugar crash. Sweetened with raw honey and coconut nectar, not a stack of syrups.
  • Real fat. Grass-fed tallow burns slow for long days.

What's inside your bar

Grass-fed goat casein

Slow-release protein. Feeds your muscles for about 7 hours instead of spiking and fading. Naturally A2-type and gentle on the gut.

Grass-fed collagen

The second protein. Rich in glycine and proline, the building blocks of your joints, skin and gut lining.

Colostrum

Rebuilds your gut. The first milk, full of growth factors. In athletes training hard in the heat, colostrum cut the gut leakiness that tough exercise causes.

Raw honey & organic coconut nectar

Natural sweeteners that bring their own minerals, enzymes and antioxidants. No sugar alcohols to ferment in your gut.

Organic chocolate chips

Real chocolate, sweetened with organic coconut sugar. The cookie in the cookie bar.

100% grass-fed beef tallow

An energy powerhouse. Dense, slow-burning fat for long days. It's mostly saturated fat, so it doesn't oxidize as easily as seed oils.

Frequently asked questions

Why did Goat Bar choose goat casein as the protein?

Goat casein is a slow-release protein, so it fuels you steadily instead of spiking and fading. It is naturally A2-type, and it is where the name comes from.

What does it taste like?

Rich and food-like. Like real chocolate chip cookie dough, sweet but not overly sweet. A satisfying, dense texture, not chalky or powdery like typical protein bars.

Will this upset my stomach?

Goat Bar has no soy, no sugar alcohols, no gums and no fiber syrups, the usual reasons protein bars sit like a brick. It does contain milk.

Why does it cost more than other bars?

Most bars are formulated to hit the lowest possible cost. Goat Bar is built on grass-fed goat casein, grass-fed collagen, real-food sweeteners and nothing synthetic. You are paying for the ingredients, not filler.

Is this good for kids?

It contains raw honey, which is not for babies under 1.

Can I eat this if I'm sensitive to dairy?

Goat Bar contains milk. Goat casein and colostrum come from milk.

Why beef tallow?

100% grass-fed beef tallow is the fat in the bar, used in place of seed oils.

Why two sweeteners, raw honey and coconut nectar?

They are the only sweeteners in the bar.
