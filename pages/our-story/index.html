Protein from goats

Why goats

Goat Bar is built around grass-fed goat casein, the protein from goat milk. Goat casein is a slow-release protein, so it fuels you steadily instead of spiking and fading, and it is naturally A2-type. Colostrum, the first milk, rounds it out. A bar that is easy on digestion, gives you energy for longer and tastes good: that is the whole idea, and goat is in the name.

Nine ingredients

Grass-fed goat casein, grass-fed bovine collagen, colostrum, organic coconut nectar, organic chocolate chips, grass-fed beef tallow, organic raw honey, organic vanilla and salt. Nothing synthetic.

Made for the climb

Pack it, pocket it, keep one in the glovebox. A bar that is easy on digestion and gives you energy for longer, all the way to the top.
